# Mind Canvas

Mind Canvas is a visual storytelling service for turning ideas into presentation-style canvas scenes.

It helps users organize thoughts, explain concepts step by step, and build narrative flows that feel more dynamic than traditional slides.

![Mind Canvas Preview](docs/preview.png)

## Overview

Mind Canvas is built for presentations that need movement, continuity, and focus.

Instead of treating a presentation as isolated pages, it treats it as a sequence of connected scenes. This makes it easier to reveal ideas gradually, keep audience attention, and show how concepts evolve over time.

It is especially useful for:

- concept presentations
- startup and investor decks
- technical architecture walkthroughs
- education and explainer content
- AI-assisted presentation creation

## Features

- Step-by-step storytelling on a canvas
- Smooth scene transitions across multiple steps
- Visual composition with text, blocks, and arrows
- Presentation mode for guided walkthroughs
- Fast save, load, and reuse of presentation scenes
- AI-friendly content flow for generating visual narratives from prompts

## What Makes It Useful

Most slide tools are optimized for static pages.
Mind Canvas is optimized for narrative continuity.

That means you can:

- keep a story visually connected from step to step
- reveal information gradually instead of showing everything at once
- guide the audience through a concept with stronger focus
- turn one idea into a clear visual sequence quickly

## Typical Use Cases

- Founders explaining a product or market story
- Operators aligning a team around strategy or process
- Educators teaching a concept in stages
- Designers mapping a user journey or service flow
- Anyone who wants a more visual and interactive alternative to ordinary slides

## Experience

Mind Canvas is designed to feel simple in use:

- create or load a visual story
- arrange content on the canvas
- move step by step through the narrative
- refine and reuse the result

The goal is not just to place objects on a screen.
The goal is to help people explain ideas clearly.

## AI Workflow

Mind Canvas also fits an AI-assisted workflow.
You can generate structured scenes from a prompt, load them, refine them visually, and reuse them as polished presentation material.

This makes it useful for teams that want to move from rough thinking to presentable output much faster.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

### Tools

- `Q`: select
- `W`: box
- `E`: text
- `R`: arrow

### Edit

- `Cmd/Ctrl + C`: copy selection
- `Cmd/Ctrl + X`: cut selection
- `Cmd/Ctrl + D`: duplicate selection
- `Backspace`: delete selection
- `Cmd/Ctrl + Z`: undo
- `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + Z`: redo

### Steps and View

- `1` or `Left`: previous step
- `2` or `Right`: next step
- `Down`: jump to first step
- `Up`: jump to last step
- `H`: toggle previous-step overlay
- `P`: presentation mode
- `F`: focus selected nodes
- `C`: reset viewport to origin
- `F1`: shortcut help

## Focus

Mind Canvas is ultimately a service for better communication.
It exists to help people structure thinking, present more clearly, and move from idea to explanation with less friction.
